| 2026-08-30 | Phase A executed; found and fixed a second, more consequential `git-guardrail.sh` gap (global hook, not scoped to this repo) while committing the roadmap's CI-workflow task | `.github/workflows/rust-ci.yml` (Phase A Task #16, cargo test/clippy/audit on the pinned toolchain) committed to `agent/claude/rust-ci-workflow` without the expected protected-path block. | 2026-08-30 | Phase A.8 resolved: the held-out-seed grading model's trust-boundary contradiction, via three doubt-driven-development cycles (single-model cycle 1 + independently-convergent Codex cross-model, single-model cycles 2 and 3) | Full revision in `docs/workshop-design.md`'s deterministic-gate section, point 2-3.
